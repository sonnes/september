Present — look & colour options

Three directions for the presentation surface, replacing the retired editorial reel look (Playfair serif on dark Tailwind pairs, grain, vignette). The chosen look skins both the live Present overlay and the exported 9:16 video. Everything else is unchanged from the approved UX: chunking rules, segmented progress, 44px close and speaker controls, tap zones, keys, reduced-motion behaviour. Word states everywhere: already-spoken words dim to 55%, the active word carries the accent underline.

Decision (2026-08-24): Option A is the default — Indigo, Ink, and Paper tones — and Option B's four paper tints join the same picker as further choices. One seven-tone system, two families. Option C is not pursued; Lexend stays brand-only.

Option A — Keycap Recommended

The presentation is September itself, loud. Noto Sans only — bold display chunks, calm support chunks — on three tones: Indigo (the brand, glows in a dim room), Ink (near-black, maximum text glow), Paper (white, for bright rooms and glare). No grain, no vignette, no decoration: the quiet-utility thesis at display size. The Sep keycap mark signs the corner instead of a watermark.

Option B — Reading light Warm

A printed page, not a video. Light paper tints with near-black ink — the highest-legibility polarity in daylight and the least like social media. Display chunks in Fraunces, a warm modern serif (replacing Playfair); support chunks in Noto Sans. Each tint pairs with one deep accent for the active word. A small ink wordmark signs the corner.

Option C — Marquee Maximum reach

A departure board: near-black screen, enormous letters, one electric accent. Built for distance, low vision, and dim rooms — the strongest "read me across the room" of the three. Set in Lexend, the typeface designed for reading proficiency — a deliberate, scoped extension of its brand-only rule, the way Playfair was scoped to reels. The accent is the user's choice; the display text carries it, support text stays white.

Recommendation — A. It spans bright and dim rooms with its three tones, adds no fonts and no new palette system, makes the exported video unmistakably September, and is the only option that is pure DESIGN.md — calm, dignified, one confident accent. B is the pick if Present should feel like a keepsake; C if maximum distance-legibility outweighs warmth. A user-facing tone/accent switch exists in every option, so no room is left behind either way.

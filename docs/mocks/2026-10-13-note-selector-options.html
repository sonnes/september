Note selector options

Today the note tabs are a row of pills above the page. With more than four notes, the row scrolls sideways and long names are cut. About sits in the same row as the notes. Each option below is shown on a phone, the compact desktop panel, and the web app at the iPad landscape size. The frames are live: press the controls to open the selector.

A · Title menuB · Notes listC · Tabs and All notesDecisions

A · Title menu

The title of the note is the selector. A press on the title opens a list of the notes, with search, New note, and About. The list stays open until the user picks a row, so a dwell does not close it. The page has no row of tabs.

For
  • The most room for the note. No chrome above it.
  • One place to find a note on every device.
  • The list shows every note, with its first line and its time.
Against
  • Two presses to change notes.
  • The other notes are out of sight. A user does not see that more notes exist, other than the count.

B · Notes list Recommended

The notes are a list, and the note opens beside it or in its place. On a wide screen, the list is a column at the left and the note fills the rest. On the phone and the compact panel, Notes opens on the list, and a note opens on the full screen with a Notes back key. About is the first row of the list.

For
  • One model on every device: a list, then a note.
  • Large rows with a name, a first line, and a time. Names are not cut.
  • On a wide screen, any note is one press away.
  • About is apart from the notes, and still in the list.
Against
  • On a narrow screen, the change of note is two presses: Notes, then the note.
  • The column takes 280 points of the wide screen.

C · Tabs and All notes

The tabs stay for the three most recent notes. An All notes key opens a sheet of note cards. About moves to an info key beside the title. This is the nearest option to the screen of today.

For
  • Small change from today.
  • The recent notes are one press away on every device.
Against
  • Two places to find a note: the tabs and the sheet.
  • The phone has room for only one or two tabs.
  • Tab names are still cut.

Decisions for you

  • The option. My choice is B. It is the same model on every device, its rows are the largest targets, and a wide screen gets one-press switching.
  • Where Notes opens on a narrow screen (B). On the list, or on the last note with the back key. My choice is the last note, because a user who writes one note at a time then needs no extra press.
  • The order of the list. The most recent change first, as today. Pinning a note to the top is an option for later.

BehaviorRule
LevelThe panel floats above normal windows and above full-screen apps. It shows on every desktop.
FocusThe panel takes keystrokes without making September the active app. The call app keeps its menu bar and its focus state.
PositionThe user drags the header. The panel snaps to the left or right edge within 24 points. September keeps the position and size for each display, as the window does today.
SizeWidth 400 to 560 points. Height 560 points to the full screen height. Below 640 points of height, the history hides first, then the word row wraps to one line.
HideEsc with an empty draft, or the global key, hides the panel. The draft stays. Closing never discards words.
Global keyControl-Option-Space shows and hides the panel from any app. The user can change it in Settings.
DockWith the float on, September has no Dock icon. With the float off, and during setup, September has a Dock icon. A click on the Dock icon shows the panel.
AppearanceThe panel follows DESIGN.md, not the system: Noto Sans, zinc, and one indigo accent. The header stays indigo in dark mode, as the sidebar does.

Open Questions

Overlap with apps/keyboard

The macOS keyboard already floats and types into other apps. This panel speaks. Two floating September windows on one screen compete for the same edge. Options: keep them separate, or let the keyboard dock under the panel as one column.

Selection as context

Shift twice sends a selection of another app into the suggestion prompt. That text leaves the Mac when the writing service is in the cloud. The mock shows no consent step beyond the Accessibility grant. A per-capture note, or a setting, is possible.

Mood keys folded into a menu

The menu adds one press for each mood change. The alternative is a second tools row with all five keys, which costs 52 points of height.

Eye-gaze and switch users

A 440-point panel with 44-point targets fits dwell input, but rows are close together. A "Large" panel size of 560 points with 56-point rows is a possible setting.

Leaning: M2 dock split — one navigation zone at the bottom edge

Space switch and mode switch are the same kind of act (choosing where you are); putting both on the bottom edge makes a single nav zone one glance below the composer, and the header goes calm. The width tension is real but manageable at three modes + ~4 visible spaces (older spaces overflow into "＋/…"). If dock crowding tests badly, M1 (header) is the safe fallback — zero new patterns. M3 is the most elegant idea but its targets fail the 44px floor without significant enlarging; M4 is worth remembering if a future feature adds a fourth mode; M5 stops being navigation and becomes product philosophy — pair it with alternative C's drawer if the agent turns out to be more tool than place.

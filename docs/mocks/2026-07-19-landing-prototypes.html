Landing redesign — every feature is a working prototype

Proposed structure and copy for the September home page. The organizing idea: don't describe the product — hand it over. The current page has one live demo and five skeleton screenshots; this redesign gives every feature its own small, real, touchable prototype. Everything interactive below actually works (speech uses your browser's voices) — try each one.

What changes vs. the current page

  • Features section (5 skeleton cards) is dissolved into five full feature sections, each pairing copy with a live mini-prototype: phrases & codes, spaces, voice, notes, reels.
  • How-it-works (numbered 1-2-3 cards) is dropped. The hero demo teaches the same three steps by doing, and each prototype carries a one-line "Try it" hint instead.
  • The live demo moves up into the flagship "Talk" section directly under the hero — the first thing after the promise is the product itself, now with real text-to-speech.
  • Privacy gets its own indigo band (currently a footnote line in the hero). It's a differentiator; it earns a section.
  • Setup choices, final CTA, and footer keep their current copy source (SETUP_MODES) and roughly their current shape.
  • Page rhythm: white ↔ zinc-50 alternating sections, copy/prototype sides alternating, one indigo band mid-page and the indigo hero on top.
Implementation note: the "Talk" prototype is today's LiveDemoSection plus real speech via the existing speech provider. Each smaller prototype reuses app primitives (SuggestionStripes, seed phrases from DEFAULT_SPACE_SEED) rather than bespoke marketing code. The phrase-codes demo depends on the approved 2026-07-18-phrase-codes-starters-corpus plan — it ships with that feature, or the section launches with pinned phrases only.

Saved phrases & codes

Your everyday sentences, two letters away.

Save the sentences you say all the time and give them short codes. Type ty and "Thank you" surfaces instantly — one tap swaps it in. Pinned phrases stay ready before you type anything, and September quietly suggests new ones from how you talk.

Type  ty ,  hlp , or  iwb  — then tap the phrase.

Copy & behavior decisions (for review)

  • Hero headline kept ("Faster Communication / Fewer Keystrokes") — it's established and on-thesis. New supporting line "Everything on this page is the real thing — try it as you scroll" sets the contract for the prototypes.
  • Every section = eyebrow + H2 + one-paragraph lede + one "Try it" pill. No bullet lists in feature copy; the prototype carries the detail.
  • "Try it" hints are text, not hover — no hover-gated affordances, per DESIGN.md.
  • Speech uses speechSynthesis in this mock; the real page should use the same speech provider as the app (browser voice by default) so the demo is the product.
  • All targets ≥44px, visible focus rings, reduced-motion respected (bubble animation only).
  • Reel prototype uses the scoped editorial deviation (Playfair display on stone-950) — matches reel-theme.ts, loaded only for that frame.
  • Section order argument: Talk first (the product's job), then the two "fewer keystrokes" multipliers (codes, spaces), then identity (voice), then long-form (notes → reels), then trust (privacy), then choice (setup), then ask (CTA).
  • Open question: ship the phrase-codes section with the codes demo (depends on the phrase-codes plan) or launch with pinned-phrases-only copy and add codes when the feature lands.

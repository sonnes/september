The right panel, part by part

From-scratch redesign of the panel's contents. The question each tab must answer: what does someone need at arm's reach while speaking? Anything else belongs in Settings or, later, the Agent.

Frequency-orderedRail order = how often you reach mid-conversation: History, Phrases, Voice. Settings taxonomy doesn't get a vote.
Rows act on tapEvery list row's tap is its primary verb — replay, insert. Management (pin, delete) is secondary iconography.
Serves the composerEach tab either feeds the composer (History, Phrases) or tunes how it sounds (Voice). If it does neither, it's not a tab.
Live controls ≠ identitySliders you nudge mid-talk (speed, volume) sit on top; choices you make once (which voice) sit below, behind one card.
Same panel everywhereIdentical tabs in Talk and Notes. "Insert" targets whatever the composer feeds — a message or the open note.

Summary of the from-scratch shape

Three tabs, one action: History · Phrases · Voice — Display. The merge that matters is Voice: today's Provider/Voice/Speech are three tabs onto one settings form; mid-conversation nobody chooses an engine — they nudge speed, fix volume, occasionally swap voice. So the tab is ordered by that reality: live sliders first, identity card second, tuning folded, provider demoted to a filter inside the voice picker; the output device leaves the panel entirely and sits beside Speak. History and Phrases both become "rows that act": tap inserts or replays, management is secondary. Context stops being panel furniture altogether — it's the space's own "About" note, edited in the full editor, read by the suggestion engine, and later maintained by the Agent in plain sight. When Agent absorbs Phrases too, the rail is History · Voice + Display. Implementation impact: `ChatPanelTab` becomes `'history' | 'phrases' | 'voice'`; `ContextTab` is replaced by a context-kind note; `SpeechSettings` gets a `variant="live"` composition instead of `section` props.
